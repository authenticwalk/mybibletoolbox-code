KEY INSIGHTS
================================================================================

1. INFERENCE DEPENDENCY
   - 18.8% of verses mark _implicit information
   - 6% explicitly mark _implicitNecessary items
   - Suggests readers must actively interpret/supply meaning

2. GRAMMATICAL RECONSTRUCTION HEAVY
   - 87.5% of verses use square brackets
   - Relative clauses dominate (52% of bracketed verses)
   - Multi-level nesting common (43% of verses)

3. AGENT TRACKING CENTRAL
   - 52.5% of verses identify specific participants
   - 15 distinct agent types tracked
   - Person/group identification priority

4. TRANSLATION VARIABILITY ACKNOWLEDGED
   - 20.1% of verses mark semantic alternatives
   - followers/disciples pair dominates (39x, 8.4% of verses)
   - Shows awareness of translation/interpretation gaps

5. SEMANTIC UNIT CREATION
   - 166+ verses use hyphenated patterns
   - 24 distinct semantic linking patterns
   - Son-of (22x) and implicit-situational (19x) most critical

6. DISCOURSE STRUCTURE EXPLICIT
   - Document structure marked with dashes
   - Paragraph boundaries clearly marked
   - Section headings integrated
