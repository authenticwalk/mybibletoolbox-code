## Quick Lookup Table

**Need to track who's doing something?**
→ Look for `(agent-name)` in parentheses

**Need to understand implied information?**
→ Look for `_implicit`, `_implicitNecessary`, `_implicitActiveAgent`

**Need to parse complex sentence structure?**
→ Check bracket nesting depth (may reach 3-4 levels)

**Need translation alternatives?**
→ Look for `/` pairs like `followers/disciples`

**Need to understand semantic units?**
→ Look for hyphenated patterns like `in-order`, `implicit-situational`, `Son-of`

**Need to know grammatical/stylistic properties?**
→ Check parenthetical markers like `(imp)`, `(literal)`, `(dynamic)`

**Need to understand temporal/causal relationships?**
→ Look for `[When...]` patterns
