## 15 CORE PATTERNS (3+ Examples Each)

### 1. IMPLICIT-CONTEXT-FRAME
- **Pattern**: `[bracketed information]`
- **Frequency**: 150+ instances (~30% of verses)
- **Examples**:
  - Genesis 39:5: `[While Joseph was the leader...]`
  - 2 Samuel 3:36: `[David make that promise]`
  - Genesis 31:21: `[After David left Hebron]`

### 2. SUBJECT-ROLE-PARENTHETICAL
- **Pattern**: `(subject-type)` or `(imp)`
- **Frequency**: 200+ instances (~40% of verses)
- **Examples**:
  - Row 4: `I(David) (implicit-info) was not able`
  - Row 17: `(imp) You(soldiers) do not stay`
  - Row 26: `you(Ruth) (imp) watch Boaz`

### 3. RELATIVE-CLAUSE-BRACKET
- **Pattern**: `[who/that/which CLAUSE]`
- **Frequency**: 100+ instances (~20% of verses)
- **Examples**:
  - Genesis 39:5: `[who worked in Potiphar's house]`
  - Genesis 10:24: `[who was the father of Eber]`
  - Mark 7:1: `[who teach the religious laws]`

### 4. PURPOSE-CLAUSE-MARKER
- **Pattern**: `in-order-to [ACTION]`
- **Frequency**: 60+ instances (~12% of verses)
- **Examples**:
  - 2 Samuel 13:24: `[in order to cut the fur from sheep]`
  - Genesis 45:5: `[in-order-to save people]`
  - Joshua 22:26: `[to burn sacrifices on this altar]`

### 5. TEMPORAL-FRAME-CLAUSE
- **Pattern**: `[When/After/Before ACTION]`
- **Frequency**: 80+ instances (~16% of verses)
- **Examples**:
  - 2 Samuel 5:13: `[After David left Hebron]`
  - Genesis 7:16: `[just-like God promised Noah]`
  - 1 Samuel 4:13: `[When that man arrived in Shiloh]`

### 6. TRANSLATION-SLASH-ALTERNATIVE
- **Pattern**: `word1/word2[/word3]`
- **Frequency**: 100+ instances (~25% of verses)
- **Examples**:
  - followers/disciples (44 instances)
  - laws/Law (8 instances)
  - woman/witch (3 instances)

### 7. CAUSAL-FRAME-CLAUSE
- **Pattern**: `[because REASON]`
- **Frequency**: 50+ instances (~10% of verses)
- **Examples**:
  - Genesis 45:5: `[because you(brothers) sold me(Joseph)]`
  - 2 Samuel 22:21: `[because I(David) did good things]`
  - Acts 15:10: `[by putting a heavy burden on people]`

### 8. CONDITIONAL-FRAME-CLAUSE
- **Pattern**: `[If CONDITION]`
- **Frequency**: 40+ instances (~8% of verses)
- **Examples**:
  - Nehemiah 13:21: `[If you(people) stay near the wall again]`
  - Genesis 47:15: `[if we(people) do not eat]`
  - 1 Samuel 28:9: `[If I(woman) talk to a spirit]`

### 9. IMPERATIVE-SUBJECT-MARKER
- **Pattern**: `SUBJECT(imp) VERB`
- **Frequency**: 144 instances (~29% of verses)
- **Examples**:
  - Genesis 24:4: `You(servant) (imp) return`
  - Genesis 45:5: `You(brothers) (imp) do not be afraid`
  - Ruth 3:4: `you(Ruth) (imp) watch Boaz`

### 10. LITERAL-DYNAMIC-TRANSLATION
- **Pattern**: `(literal) X ... (dynamic) Y`
- **Frequency**: 37 paired instances (~7% of verses)
- **Examples**:
  - Mark 1:25: `(literal) (imp) become quiet! (dynamic) (imp) stop talking!`
  - Mark 6:53: `(literal) [After Jesus went...] (dynamic) Jesus came to shore`

### 11. RHETORICAL-QUESTION-FRAME
- **Pattern**: `(rhetorical) [QUESTION?]`
- **Frequency**: 8+ instances (~3-5% of verses)
- **Examples**:
  - Matthew 21:46: `(rhetorical) Why should you(Jairus) continue to annoy the teacher?`
  - Matthew 9:14: `So _implicit why do your(Jesus's) followers/disciples not fast?`

### 12. NAMED-ENTITY-APPOSITIVE
- **Pattern**: `named X` or `[which is in X]`
- **Frequency**: 30+ instances (~6% of verses)
- **Examples**:
  - 1 Samuel 19:19: `David is in Naioth [which is in Ramah]`
  - Genesis 35:27: `[which is near Hebron]`

### 13. SEMANTIC-ROLE-SUFFIX
- **Pattern**: `verb-LETTER` (-A, -B, -C, -D)
- **Frequency**: 5+ instances (~1-2% of clauses)
- **Examples**:
  - Matthew 27:33: `Golgotha means-C the place of bones`
  - Mark 7:7: `teach-B religious customs`

### 14. IMPLICIT-BACKGROUND-INFO
- **Pattern**: `(implicit-info)` or `(implicit-situational)`
- **Frequency**: 40+ instances (~8% of verses)
- **Examples**:
  - 2 Samuel 5:13: `At that time (implicit-info) important people often had many wives`
  - 2 Samuel 18:10: `Absalom hanging in that tree. So that soldier said to Joab`

### 15. STRUCTURAL-SECTION-MARKER
- **Pattern**: `(title)`, `(paragraph)`, `(footnote)`, `(comment-begin/end)`
- **Frequency**: 111 instances
- **Examples**:
  - Title: `(title) Saul talks to a woman/witch at Endor`
  - Paragraph: `(paragraph) [After Jesus and followers...]`
  - Footnote: `(footnote) Most manuscripts do not include verse 44`

## PATTERN NESTING EXAMPLE

Complex multi-pattern verse:
```
Acts 15:10
[So you(Jews) do not (imp) cause [God to be angry] [by putting a heavy burden
on people [who believe in Jesus] [and who are not Jews]. Even we(Jews) and
our (Jews') parents were not able to carry that burden]. So (alt) you (Jews)
do not (imp) cause [God to be angry] [by giving a difficult rule to people
[who believe in Jesus] [and who are not Jews]. Even we (Jews) and our (Jews')
parents were not able to follow those rules!]

Patterns:
- Brackets (5 levels): subordinate clauses
- (alt): alternative interpretation
- (Jews), (parents): explicit subject roles
- (imp): imperative mood
- [who CLAUSE]: relative clauses
- [by CLAUSE]: causal/instrumental
```
