## Key Distinctions

**Mood vs. Illocutionary Force**:
- **Mood** (form): Grammatical category (indicative, subjunctive, imperative)
- **Illocutionary Force** (function): Pragmatic action (declare, command, question)
- Example: "Can you pass the salt?" = Interrogative mood, Directive force (request)

**TBTA Annotates Function** (what utterance does), not just form.

## Critical Translation Cases

### ⚠️ HIGH STAKES: Genesis 1:3 - "Let There Be Light"

- **Hebrew**: יְהִי אוֹר (yehi 'or) - 3rd person JUSSIVE
- **Force**: Jussive (divine performative command)
- **Doctrine**: Creation ex nihilo by divine speech
- **FORBIDDEN**: Declarative ("there will be light") - reduces to prediction, not divine fiat

**Target Language Examples**:
- Japanese: 光あれ (hikari are) - imperative of "be"
- Mandarin: 要有光 (yào yǒu guāng) - modal 要 + "have light"
- Spanish: Haya luz - subjunctive mood

### ⚠️ CRITICAL: Genesis 1:26 - "Let Us Make Man" (Trinity)

- **Hebrew**: נַעֲשֶׂה (na'aseh) - 1st person plural COHORTATIVE
- **Force**: Jussive/Hortative (NOT imperative)
- **Doctrine**: Trinity (Father, Son, Spirit consulting)
- **Clusivity**: Use INCLUSIVE pronoun (Tagalog **tayo**, NOT kami)
- **Number**: Use TRIAL if available (exactly 3); FORBIDDEN: dual (heretical)

### ⚠️ HIGH: Exodus 20:13 - "You Shall Not Murder"

- **Hebrew**: לֹא תִּרְצָח (lo tirtzach) - PROHIBITION
- **Force**: Imperative (absolute moral command)
- **FORBIDDEN**: Declarative ("you won't murder") - loses moral authority

## Target Language Families

### MANDATORY Marking (35-40%)

**Sentence-Final Particles**:
- **East Asian**: Japanese か/だ, Korean -습니까/-다, Mandarin 吗/吧
- **Austronesian**: Tagalog ba, Cebuano ba
- **Must insert particles** even if source lacks them

### OPTIONAL Marking (50-55%)

**Word Order/Intonation**:
- **Indo-European**: English, Spanish, Russian
- **Niger-Congo**: Swahili, Bantu languages
- **Can use intonation alone**

**Key Insight**: 80% of languages use **explicit marking** (particles/morphology). Only 18% use intonation alone.

## Theological Classification

**Non-Arbitrary (15%)**:
- Divine fiats (Gen 1) - Jussive essential
- Trinitarian cohortatives (Gen 1:26) - 1st plural hortative
- Ten Commandments - Imperative force
- Great Commission (Matt 28:19) - Imperative authority
- Blessings vs. commands - Interpretation affects ethics

**Arbitrary (85%)**:
- Narrative declaratives (70%)
- Routine questions (8%)
- Mundane commands (10%)
- Dialogue (7%)
