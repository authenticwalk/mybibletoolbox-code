# Edge Cases: When NOT to Use Indicative

**Purpose**: Identify patterns where Indicative (97% default) is NOT the correct mood.

## Summary

In TBTA, 97.21% of mood labels are Indicative. This document analyzes the 2,010 non-indicative cases to find predictive patterns.

## Non-Indicative Categories

### 1. 'may' (permissive) - 150 cases (0.21%)

**Pattern**: Permission granted for an action

**Recognizable by**:
- Questions "may we...?"
- Statements of allowance "you may eat"
- Conditional permissions "if X, then Y may..."

**Examples**:
- EXO.012.016: "Israelite **prepare** meal" - permitted activity
- GEN.024.023: "servant **sleep** in house" - asking permission

**Key insight**: Often involves hospitality, dietary laws, or ritual permissions.

### 2. 'might' Potential - 367 cases (0.51%)

**Pattern**: Uncertain possibility, hypothetical, or speculative

**Recognizable by**:
- Fear of what could happen
- Conditional scenarios
- Questions about possibility

**Examples**:
- GEN.020.011: "they **might** kill me" - fear
- 1KI.008.037: "if person **be** sick" - conditional
- EXO.021.26: "slave **be** able see" - capability

**Key insight**: Often in conditional clauses or expressing fear/doubt.

### 3. 'must' Obligation - 633 cases (0.88%)

**Pattern**: Strong necessity, required action, divine command

**Recognizable by**:
- Legal requirements in law codes
- Consequences requiring response
- Divine mandates

**Examples**:
- EXO.021.12: "Israelite **kill** person" - legal consequence (death penalty)
- EXO.012.06: "all person Israel **kill** young sheep" - Passover requirement
- JOS.001.14: "person **help** relative" - tribal obligation

**Key insight**: Often in legal/ritual contexts with explicit obligation.

### 4. 'should' Obligation - 567 cases (0.79%)

**Pattern**: Moderate advice, ethical guidance, recommendation

**Recognizable by**:
- Ethical teaching
- Questions seeking guidance ("shall I...?")
- Recommendations

**Examples**:
- TIT.002.10: "slave **show** master faithful" - ethical expectation
- 1SA.023.02: "David **attack** Philistines?" - seeking guidance
- 1TH.005.14: "person **work**" - exhortation

**Key insight**: Often in wisdom literature, epistles, or seeking divine guidance.

### 5. 'should not' Obligation - 158 cases (0.22%)

**Pattern**: Negative advice, discouraged behavior

**Recognizable by**:
- Rhetorical questions implying "no"
- Warnings against behavior
- Gentle prohibitions

**Examples**:
- NEH.006.11: "man **run-away**?" - rhetorical "should I flee?"
- JON.004.09: "Jonah **be** angry plant?" - rhetorical criticism
- JHN.002.04: "Mary **ask** problem Jesus" - implied "don't"

**Key insight**: Often rhetorical questions or gentle corrections.

### 6. Forbidden Obligation - 121 cases (0.17%)

**Pattern**: Absolute prohibition, divine command NOT to do

**Recognizable by**:
- Ten Commandments style prohibitions
- "Do not..." commands
- Strict warnings with consequences

**Examples**:
- EXO.020.05: "Israelite **bow** idol" - idolatry prohibition
- MRK.003.12: "spirit **tell** Jesus" - command to demons
- JOS.023.07: "Israelite **bow** god" - pagan worship banned

**Key insight**: Strong prohibitions, often in legal/covenant contexts.

### 7. Definite/Probable/Unlikely Potential - 14 cases total

**Rare categories** - likely context-dependent:
- Definite: "will surely happen"
- Probable: "probably will"
- Unlikely: "doubtful" (JHN.004.33 "could someone have brought?")

## Decision Tree for Non-Indicative

```
Is there an explicit prohibition (shall not, do not, forbidden)?
├─ YES → Is it strong/divine command? → Forbidden Obligation
│        Is it soft/advice? → 'should not' Obligation
│
Is there obligation/requirement?
├─ YES → Is it mandatory (law, consequence)? → 'must' Obligation
│        Is it advisory (should, ought)? → 'should' Obligation
│
Is there permission/allowance?
├─ YES → 'may' (permissive)
│
Is there uncertainty/possibility?
├─ YES → 'might' Potential
│
Otherwise → Indicative (default)
```

## Contextual Signals

| Signal | Likely Mood |
|--------|-------------|
| "Do not..." | Forbidden Obligation |
| "You shall not..." | Forbidden Obligation |
| "You should/ought to..." | 'should' Obligation |
| "You must..." | 'must' Obligation |
| "Might/could happen" | 'might' Potential |
| "May I...?" / "You may..." | 'may' (permissive) |
| Law code sections | 'must' or Forbidden |
| Wisdom/ethical teaching | 'should' family |
| Fear expressions | 'might' Potential |
| Questions seeking guidance | 'should' Obligation |
