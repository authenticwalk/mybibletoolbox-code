# Semantic Role Reason Groups

Logical and theological categories for grouping semantic role assignments.

## Theological Groups (HIGH STAKES)

### TRINITY
- Divine persons as Agents in creation (Gen 1, John 1)
- God/Father/Son/Spirit roles in divine action
- Preventing heretical role assignments (Arianism, Subordinationism)

### CHRISTOLOGY-DUAL-NATURE
- Christ as both Agent (divine will) and Patient (human suffering)
- Passion narratives, sacrifice passages
- Preventing one-nature heresies (Docetism, Arianism)

### ATONEMENT
- Father as Agent (sender), Son as Agent+Patient (offering/offered)
- Sacrifice mechanics: priest/victim/altar roles
- Beneficiary (humanity) vs Agent (God initiates)

### PRAYER-MEDIATION
- Addressee of prayer (Father/Son/Spirit)
- Mediator role (Christ as Instrument)
- vs. saints as intercessors (not ultimate Addressees)

###SALVATION-SOVEREIGNTY
- God as Agent (initiates salvation)
- Humanity as Patient/Beneficiary (receives)
- Faith as Instrument (means, not cause)
- vs. Pelagianism (human as Agent)

## Linguistic/Grammatical Groups

### PROPER-NAME
- Named individuals/places as Agents or Patients
- Discourse tracking with specific participants

### PRONOUN
- Pronominal Agents/Patients
- Potential ambiguity in referent tracking

### COLLECTIVE
- Groups, crowds, nations as Agents/Patients
- Plural entities with unified action

### ABSTRACT
- Abstract nouns as Agents (personification) or Patients
- Love, wisdom, truth, etc.

### BODY-PART
- Body parts as Instruments or Patients
- Merismus (hand, foot representing whole person)

## Verb Frame Groups

### MOTION-VERB
- Source: starting point of motion
- Destination: endpoint of motion
- Path Instruments

### TRANSFER-VERB
- Source: giver
- Destination: receiver
- Patient: thing transferred
- Beneficiary: for whose benefit

### COMMUNICATION-VERB
- Agent: speaker
- Addressee: spoken to
- Patient: message/content
- Beneficiary: for whose benefit spoken

### MENTAL-VERB
- State (Experiencer): feeler of emotion/thought
- Patient: object of emotion/thought
- Source: cause of emotion

### CREATION-VERB
- Agent: creator
- Patient: created thing
- Instrument: means of creation
- Source: material (if specified)

### CAUSATION-VERB
- Agent: causer
- Patient: caused thing/event
- Instrument: means of causation

## Contextual/Arbitrary Groups

### NARRATIVE-CLARITY
- Role assignment affects who-did-what comprehension
- Multiple participants, potential ambiguity
- Not theologically critical, but affects clarity

### STYLISTIC
- Multiple role options acceptable
- Source vs Destination in spatial metaphors
- Beneficiary vs Addressee ambiguity

### PASSIVE-VOICE
- Patient promoted to subject position
- Agent demoted or omitted

### AMBIGUOUS-PREPOSITION
- Hebrew ל/ב polysemy
- Greek διά/ἐν multiple senses
- Context-dependent role assignment

## Adversarial/Difficult Cases

### TRINITY-ADVERSARIAL
- Verses easily misinterpreted to support heresy
- Requires careful Agent/Patient/Instrument distinction

### VOLITION-AMBIGUOUS
- Unclear if action is voluntary (Agent) or coerced (Patient)
- Critical for free will, divine sovereignty debates

### DUAL-ROLE
- Same entity is both Agent and Patient in verse
- Reflexive actions, reciprocal verbs

### IMPLICIT-AGENT
- Agent unexpressed but inferable
- Passive divine (God as implicit Agent)

### METAPHORICAL
- Figurative language obscures literal roles
- Personification of abstract entities

## Usage Guidelines

1. **Choose ONE primary reason_group** per entry
2. **Theological groups override linguistic groups** when both apply
3. **Use ADVERSARIAL suffix** for theologically critical verses easily misunderstood
4. **Document ambiguity** when multiple groups equally valid

## Examples

- GEN-001-001 "God created" → `TRINITY` (Agent role critical)
- JHN-001-003 "through him" → `CHRISTOLOGY-DUAL-NATURE` or `TRINITY-ADVERSARIAL`
- JHN-003-016 "God gave" → `ATONEMENT`
- MAT-006-009 "pray to Father" → `PRAYER-MEDIATION`
- ROM-003-024 "justified by grace" → `SALVATION-SOVEREIGNTY`
- MAT-005-003 "blessed are" → `STATE` (mental verb, arbitrary participant)
- GEN-012-001 "Abram" → `PROPER-NAME`
