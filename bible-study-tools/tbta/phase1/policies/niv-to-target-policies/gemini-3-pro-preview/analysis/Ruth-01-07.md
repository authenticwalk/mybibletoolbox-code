# Analysis of Ruth 1:7

**Source (NIV)**: "With her two daughters-in-law she left the place where she had been living and set out on the road that would take them back to the land of Judah."

**Target (Unchurched Adults)**: "Then Naomi and her two daughters-in-law left the place where they were living and started walking on the road that led to Judah."

## Transformations Identified

1.  **Unified Subject**:
    - "With her two daughters-in-law she left" -> "Naomi and her two daughters-in-law left". (Compound subject used for physical movement).
2.  **Concrete Action**:
    - "set out on the road" -> "started walking on the road".
3.  **Simplification**:
    - "road that would take them back to..." -> "road that led to...".
