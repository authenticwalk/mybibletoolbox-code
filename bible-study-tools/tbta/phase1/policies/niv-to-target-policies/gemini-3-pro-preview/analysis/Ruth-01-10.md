# Analysis of Ruth 1:10

**Source (NIV)**: "and said to her, “We will go back with you to your people.”"

**Target (Unchurched Adults)**: "Then Naomi's daughters-in-law said to her, “We'll certainly go to your people with you.”"

## Transformations Identified

1.  **Participant Explicitation**:
    - "said to her" -> "Naomi's daughters-in-law said to her".
2.  **Emphasis**:
    - "We will go back" -> "We'll certainly go". (Captures Hebrew emphatic construction).
3.  **Direction**:
    - "go back" -> "go". (Since they are Moabites, they aren't technically "returning" to Judah, just going there. "Return" is only true for Naomi. TBTA fixes this logical precision).
