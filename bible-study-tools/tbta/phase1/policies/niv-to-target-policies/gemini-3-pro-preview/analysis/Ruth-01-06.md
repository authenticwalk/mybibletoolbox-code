# Analysis of Ruth 1:6

**Source (NIV)**: "When Naomi heard in Moab that the Lord had come to the aid of his people by providing food for them, she and her daughters-in-law prepared to return home from there."

**Target (Unchurched Adults)**: "While Naomi was living in Moab, she heard that the LORD was taking care of his people who were in Israel. The LORD was giving food to those people. So Naomi prepared to return to Bethlehem. Naomi's daughters-in-law also prepared to go to Bethlehem with her."

## Transformations Identified

1.  **Temporal Backgrounding**:
    - "When Naomi heard in Moab" -> "While Naomi was living in Moab, she heard..." (Explicating "living").
2.  **Deconstruct Complex Predicates**:
    - "come to the aid... by providing food" -> Two sentences:
      1.  "LORD was taking care of his people..."
      2.  "The LORD was giving food..."
3.  **Explicitation of Location**:
    - "his people" -> "his people who were in Israel".
    - "return home" -> "return to Bethlehem".
4.  **Subject Separation (Leader/Follower)**:
    - "she and her daughters-in-law prepared" -> "Naomi prepared... Naomi's daughters-in-law also prepared... with her."
    - This breaks the compound subject to highlight Naomi's agency and the others' following.
