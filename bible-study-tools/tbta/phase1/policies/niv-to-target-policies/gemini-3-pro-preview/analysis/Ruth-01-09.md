# Analysis of Ruth 1:9

**Source (NIV)**: "May the Lord grant that each of you will find rest in the home of another husband.” Then she kissed them goodbye and they wept aloud"

**Target (Unchurched Adults)**: "May the LORD give new husbands to you so that you can rest.” Then Naomi kissed her daughters-in-law. And Naomi and her daughters-in-law wept loudly."

## Transformations Identified

1.  **Causative restructuring**:
    - "grant that... find rest in home of husband" -> "give new husbands to you so that you can rest."
    - Logic: Having a husband -> Rest/Security.
2.  **Pronoun Disambiguation**:
    - "she kissed them" -> "Naomi kissed her daughters-in-law".
    - "they wept" -> "Naomi and her daughters-in-law wept".
3.  **Vocabulary**:
    - "wept loudly" retained (not simplified to "cried" for adults).
