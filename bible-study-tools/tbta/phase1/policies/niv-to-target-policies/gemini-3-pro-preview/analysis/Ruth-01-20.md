# Analysis of Ruth 1:20

**Source (NIV)**: "“Don’t call me Naomi,” she told them. “Call me Mara, because the Almighty has made my life very bitter."

**Target (Unchurched Adults)**: "But Naomi said to those people, “Don't call me 'Naomi'. Call me 'Mara' because the LORD Almighty caused me to become very bitter.” Footnote: Naomi means 'sweet'. But Mara means 'bitter'."

## Transformations Identified

1.  **Cultural Footnoting**:
    *   The text hinges on the meaning of names. Added footnote explaining Naomi/Sweet and Mara/Bitter.
2.  **Causative Structure**:
    *   "Made my life bitter" -> "Caused me to become very bitter".
