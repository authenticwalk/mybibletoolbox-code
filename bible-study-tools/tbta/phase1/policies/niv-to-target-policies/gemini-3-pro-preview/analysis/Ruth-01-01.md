# Analysis of Ruth 1:1

**Source (NIV)**: "In the days when the judges ruled, there was a famine in the land. So a man from Bethlehem in Judah, together with his wife and two sons, went to live for a while in the country of Moab."

**Target (Unchurched Adults)**: "Title: Elimelech and Naomi move from Bethlehem to Moab. When judges were ruling Israel, there was a famine in Israel. A certain man was from Bethlehem, which was in Judah. That man, his wife, and his two sons moved from Bethlehem to a country named Moab to live there for a few years. Footnote: Before the Israelites had kings, the judges were the leaders of Israel."

## Transformations Identified

1.  **Implicit -> Explicit**:
    *   "the land" -> "Israel".
    *   "judges" -> "judges were ruling Israel".
2.  **Sentence Splitting**:
    *   Background state ("man from Bethlehem") is separated from the main event ("moved").
    *   "A certain man was from Bethlehem..." -> New sentence.
3.  **Subject Grouping**:
    *   "man... together with his wife..." -> "That man, his wife, and his two sons moved..." (Compound subject).
4.  **Class + Name**:
    *   "country of Moab" -> "country named Moab".
5.  **Concrete Duration**:
    *   "live for a while" -> "live there for a few years".
