# Analysis of Ruth 1:8

**Source (NIV)**: "Then Naomi said to her two daughters-in-law, “Go back, each of you, to your mother’s home. May the Lord show you kindness, as you have shown kindness to your dead husbands and to me."

**Target (Unchurched Adults)**: "But Naomi said to her two daughters-in-law, “My daughters, return to your mothers' houses. May the LORD be kind to you like you were kind to your dead husbands and me."

## Transformations Identified

1.  **Vocative Insertion**:
    - Added "My daughters," to the speech.
2.  **Idiom Simplification**:
    - "show you kindness" -> "be kind to you".
    - "as you have shown" -> "like you were kind".
3.  **Optative Retention**:
    - "May the LORD..." is kept for adults (vs "I pray" for children).
4.  **Concrete Location**:
    - "mother's home" -> "mothers' houses" (Plural possessive, physical house).
