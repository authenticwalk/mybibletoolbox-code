# Analysis of Ruth 1:13

**Source (NIV)**: "would you wait until they grew up? Would you remain unmarried for them? No, my daughters. It is more bitter for me than for you, because the Lord’s hand has turned against me!"

**Target (Unchurched Adults)**: "Won't you marry other men? My daughters, I'm very bitter because the LORD treated me badly! <<If you come with me, you also will become very sad.”>>"

## Transformations Identified

1.  **Negative Rhetorical -> Explicit**:
    - "Would you remain unmarried?" -> "Won't you marry other men?" (Shifts focus to the action they _should_ take).
2.  **Metaphor to Literal**:
    - "Lord's hand has turned against me" -> "LORD treated me badly".
3.  **Logical Bridge**:
    - Added `<<If you come with me, you also will become very sad.>>`. Explains _why_ Naomi's bitterness/suffering matters to them (contagion of misfortune).
