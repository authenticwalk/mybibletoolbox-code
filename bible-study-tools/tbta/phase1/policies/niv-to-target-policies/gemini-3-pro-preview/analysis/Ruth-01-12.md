# Analysis of Ruth 1:12

**Source (NIV)**: "Return home, my daughters; I am too old to have another husband. Even if I thought there was still hope for me—even if I had a husband tonight and then gave birth to sons—"

**Target (Unchurched Adults)**: "My daughters, return to your parents' houses. I'm not able to marry another man because I'm too old. I don't hope to marry another man. Even if I marry a man tonight, and even if I give birth to sons, will you wait for them to grow up?"

## Transformations Identified

1.  **Location Specification**:
    - "Return home" -> "return to your parents' houses". (Clarifies whose home, since they are widows).
2.  **Concrete Action**:
    - "Have another husband" -> "Marry another man".
    - "Had a husband" -> "Marry a man".
3.  **Hope Explicitation**:
    - "thought there was still hope" -> "hope to marry another man". (Defines what the hope is).
