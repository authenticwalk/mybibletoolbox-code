# Analysis of Ruth 1:5

**Source (NIV)**: "both Mahlon and Kilion also died, and Naomi was left without her two sons and her husband."

**Target (Unchurched Adults)**: "Then Mahlon and Kilion also died. So Naomi didn't have her sons or her husband."

## Transformations Identified

1.  **Passive Privation -> Active Negative Possession**:
    - "was left without" -> "didn't have".
2.  **Simple Connectors**:
    - "Then...", "So..."
