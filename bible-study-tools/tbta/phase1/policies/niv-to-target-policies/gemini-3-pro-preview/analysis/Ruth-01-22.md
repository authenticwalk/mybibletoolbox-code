# Analysis of Ruth 1:22

**Source (NIV)**: "So Naomi returned from Moab accompanied by Ruth the Moabite, her daughter-in-law, arriving in Bethlehem as the barley harvest was beginning."

**Target (Unchurched Adults)**: "So Naomi returned from Moab. And Ruth, who was from Moab, went to Bethlehem with Naomi. Naomi and Ruth arrived in Bethlehem during the time when people started harvesting barley."

## Transformations Identified

1.  **Event Splitting**:
    *   Return, Accompaniment, Arrival -> Split into three distinct SVO sentences.
2.  **Temporal Explicitation**:
    *   "As the barley harvest was beginning" -> "during the time when people started harvesting barley." (Active event description).
