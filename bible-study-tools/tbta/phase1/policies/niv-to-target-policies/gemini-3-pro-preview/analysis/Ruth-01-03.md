# Analysis of Ruth 1:3

**Source (NIV)**: "Now Elimelech, Naomi’s husband, died, and she was left with her two sons."

**Target (Unchurched Adults)**: "Later Elimelech, who was Naomi's husband, died. So Naomi lived with her two sons."

## Transformations Identified

1.  **Appositive -> Relative Clause**:
    *   "Elimelech, Naomi's husband" -> "Elimelech, who was Naomi's husband".
2.  **Passive -> Active State**:
    *   "she was left with" -> "So Naomi lived with".
3.  **Temporal Connector**:
    *   "Now..." -> "Later..."
