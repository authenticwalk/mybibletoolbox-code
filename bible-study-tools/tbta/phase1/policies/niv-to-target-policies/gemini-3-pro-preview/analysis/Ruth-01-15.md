# Analysis of Ruth 1:15

**Source (NIV)**: "“Look,” said Naomi, “your sister-in-law is going back to her people and her gods. Go back with her.”"

**Target (Unchurched Adults)**: "Naomi said to Ruth, “Orpah is returning to her people and her gods. Go with Orpah.”"

## Transformations Identified

1.  **Participant Specificity**:
    *   "Sister-in-law" -> "Orpah".
    *   "Her" (at end) -> "Orpah".
2.  **Simplification**:
    *   Removed "Look".
    *   "Go back with her" -> "Go with Orpah".
