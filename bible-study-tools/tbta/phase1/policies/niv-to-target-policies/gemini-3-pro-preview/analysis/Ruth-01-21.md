# Analysis of Ruth 1:21

**Source (NIV)**: "I went away full, but the Lord has brought me back empty. Why call me Naomi? The Lord has afflicted me; the Almighty has brought misfortune upon me.”"

**Target (Unchurched Adults)**: "Then Naomi said, “When I left Bethlehem, I had many good things. But the LORD took those things away from me. So why should you call me 'Naomi'? The LORD caused me to suffer. The LORD Almighty caused many bad things to happen to me.”"

## Transformations Identified

1.  **Metaphor Deconstruction**:
    *   "Full" -> "Had many good things".
    *   "Empty" -> "Took those things away from me".
    *   This shifts from a container metaphor to a possession/privation logic.
2.  **Causative Vocabulary**:
    *   "Afflicted" -> "Caused to suffer".
    *   "Misfortune" -> "Bad things to happen".
