# Analysis of Ruth 1:17

**Source (NIV)**: "Where you die I will die, and there I will be buried. May the Lord deal with me, be it ever so severely, if even death separates you and me.”"

**Target (Unchurched Adults)**: "I'll die at the place where you'll die. And I will be buried there. I swear to the LORD that I'll stay with you until I die. If I don't keep this promise, the LORD should punish me severely.”"

## Transformations Identified

1.  **Oath Formula Logic**:
    *   Biblical Idiom: "May God do X if Y happens".
    *   Target Logic: "I swear [Positive Promise]. If I don't keep this promise, [God should punish me]."
2.  **Semantic clarity**:
    *   "if even death separates" -> "stay with you until I die". (Positive formulation of the commitment).
