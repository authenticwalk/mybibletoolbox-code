# Analysis of Ruth 1:14

**Source (NIV)**: "At this they wept aloud again. Then Orpah kissed her mother-in-law goodbye, but Ruth clung to her."

**Target (Unchurched Adults)**: "Then Naomi and her daughters-in-law wept again loudly. Then Orpah kissed Naomi and said goodbye to her. <<And Orpah left Naomi.>> But Ruth hugged Naomi."

## Transformations Identified

1.  **Implicit Event Explicitation**:
    - "Kissed goodbye" implies leaving. Added `<<And Orpah left Naomi.>>` to close the event loop.
2.  **Vocabulary**:
    - "Clung" -> "hugged". (Concrete physical action).
3.  **Participant Clarity**:
    - "Mother-in-law" -> "Naomi".
