# Analysis of Ruth 1:11

**Source (NIV)**: "Return home, my daughters. Why would you come with me? Am I going to have any more sons, who could become your husbands?"

**Target (Unchurched Adults)**: "But Naomi said, “My daughters, return to your houses. Why should you come with me? <<I won't be able to take care of you.>> Will I give birth to more sons who will become your husbands?"

## Transformations Identified

1.  **Logical Explicitation**:
    - "Why would you come with me?" implies a reason not to. Added `<<I won't be able to take care of you.>>` to make the argument explicit.
2.  **Concrete Vocabulary**:
    - "Return home" -> "Return to your houses".
    - "Have any more sons" -> "Give birth to more sons". (Biological precision).
3.  **Rhetorical Structure**:
    - Retained the rhetorical questions but bolstered them with the explicit logical connector.
