# Analysis of Ruth 1:16

**Source (NIV)**: "But Ruth replied, “Don’t urge me to leave you or to turn back from you. Where you go I will go, and where you stay I will stay. Your people will be my people and your God my God."

**Target (Unchurched Adults)**: "But Ruth said to Naomi, “Don't tell me to leave you. Allow me to go with you. I'll go to the place where you'll go. And I will stay at the place where you'll stay. Your people will become my people. And your God will become my God."

## Transformations Identified

1.  **Negative/Positive Pair**:
    *   "Don't urge me" -> "Don't tell me... Allow me...". (Splits the plea into prohibition and permission).
2.  **Place Explicitation**:
    *   "Where you go" -> "to the place where you'll go".
3.  **State Change**:
    *   "will be my people" -> "will become my people".
