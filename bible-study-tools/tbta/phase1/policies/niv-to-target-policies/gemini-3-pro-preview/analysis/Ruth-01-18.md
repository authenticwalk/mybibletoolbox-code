# Analysis of Ruth 1:18

**Source (NIV)**: "When Naomi realized that Ruth was determined to go with her, she stopped urging her."

**Target (Unchurched Adults)**: "Naomi realized that Ruth decided to go with her. So Naomi stopped arguing with Ruth."

## Transformations Identified

1.  **Psychological Simplification**:
    *   "Determined" -> "Decided".
2.  **Action Specificity**:
    *   "Urging" -> "Arguing with". (Clarifies the nature of the interaction).
3.  **Participant Reference**:
    *   "She stopped urging her" -> "Naomi stopped arguing with Ruth".
