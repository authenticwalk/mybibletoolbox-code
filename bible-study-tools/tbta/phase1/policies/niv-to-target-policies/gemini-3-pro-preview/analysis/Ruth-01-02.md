# Analysis of Ruth 1:2

**Source (NIV)**: "The man’s name was Elimelech, his wife’s name Naomi, and the names of his two sons were Mahlon and Kilion. They were Ephrathites from Bethlehem, Judah. And they went to Moab and lived there."

**Target (Unchurched Adults)**: "That man's name was Elimelech. Elimelech's wife's name was Naomi. One son's name was Mahlon. And the other son's name was Kilion. Elimelech and Naomi were in Ephrah's clan. That family was living in a town named Bethlehem, which was in <<the region named>> Judah. But they went to a country named Moab and lived there."

## Transformations Identified

1.  **List Deconstruction**:
    *   "names of his two sons were M and K" -> "One son's name was M. And the other son's name was K."
    *   Separate sentences for each family member's name.
2.  **Gentilic Expansion**:
    *   "Ephrathites" -> "in Ephrah's clan".
3.  **Geographic Specificity**:
    *   "Bethlehem, Judah" -> "town named Bethlehem, which was in <<the region named>> Judah."
4.  **Reference Management**:
    *   "They" (at start of movement) -> "That family".
5.  **Contrast**:
    *   "And they went..." -> "But they went..." (Implies leaving their home/clan is a contrast/disruption).
