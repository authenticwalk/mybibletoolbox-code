# Ruth 1:2 Analysis

## Step 1: Source Texts

### NIV
> The man's name was Elimelek, his wife's name was Naomi, and the names of his two sons were Mahlon and Kilion. They were Ephrathites from Bethlehem, Judah. And they went to Moab and lived there.

### Target Format (Unchurched Adults)
> That man's name was Elimelech. Elimelech's wife's name was Naomi. One son's name was Mahlon. And the other son's name was Kilion. Elimelech and Naomi were in Ephrah's clan. That family was living in a town named Bethlehem, which was in <<the region named>> Judah. But they went to a country named Moab and lived there.

### Target Format (Churched Adults)
> That man's name was Elimelech. Elimelech's wife's name was Naomi. One son's name was Mahlon. And the other son's name was Kilion. Elimelech and Naomi were in Ephrah's clan. That family was living in a town named Bethlehem, which was in Judah. But they went to a country named Moab and lived there.

## Step 2: Differences Identified

| NIV | Target | Type |
|-----|--------|------|
| "The man's name" | "That man's name" | Determiner: "the" → "that" (referential) |
| "Elimelek" | "Elimelech" | Spelling standardization |
| "his wife's name was Naomi" | "Elimelech's wife's name was Naomi" | Pronoun → proper noun |
| "the names of his two sons were Mahlon and Kilion" | "One son's name was Mahlon. And the other son's name was Kilion." | List → separate sentences |
| "They were Ephrathites from Bethlehem, Judah" | "Elimelech and Naomi were in Ephrah's clan. That family was living in a town named Bethlehem, which was in <<the region named>> Judah." | Pronoun resolution + expansion |
| "And they went to Moab" | "But they went to a country named Moab" | Connector: "And" → "But" + "named" construction |
| "lived there" | "lived there" | (same) |

## Step 3: Reasons (Intermediary Layer)

1. **Determiner Change**: "That man" maintains referent from previous verse
2. **Spelling Standardization**: Consistent character name spelling
3. **Pronoun Resolution**: "his" → "Elimelech's" for clarity
4. **List Decomposition**: Separate sentences for each son's name (simpler parsing)
5. **Pronoun Resolution**: "They" → "Elimelech and Naomi" explicit
6. **Expansion**: "Ephrathites" → "in Ephrah's clan" (more explicit)
7. **Geographic Expansion**: "from Bethlehem, Judah" → "living in a town named Bethlehem, which was in <<the region named>> Judah"
8. **Connector Change**: "And" → "But" (contrastive relationship)
9. **Implicit Information**: "<<the region named>>" marks implicit for some audiences
