# Ruth 1:1 Analysis

## Step 1: Source Texts

### NIV
> In the days when the judges ruled, there was a famine in the land. So a man from Bethlehem in Judah, together with his wife and two sons, went to live for a while in the country of Moab.

### Target Format (Unchurched Adults)
> Title: Elimelech and Naomi move from Bethlehem to Moab. When judges were ruling Israel, there was a famine in Israel. A certain man was from Bethlehem, which was in Judah. That man, his wife, and his two sons moved from Bethlehem to a country named Moab to live there for a few years.

### Target Format (Churched Adults)
> Title: Elimelech and Naomi move from Bethlehem to Moab. When judges were ruling Israel, there was a famine in Israel. A certain man was from Bethlehem, which was in Judah. That man, his wife, and his two sons moved from Bethlehem to a country named Moab to live there for a few years.

## Step 2: Differences Identified

| NIV | Target | Type |
|-----|--------|------|
| "In the days when the judges ruled" | "When judges were ruling Israel" | Temporal clause simplification |
| "there was a famine in the land" | "there was a famine in Israel" | Specificity: "land" → "Israel" |
| "So a man" | "A certain man" | Determiner: "a" → "a certain" |
| "from Bethlehem in Judah" | "from Bethlehem, which was in Judah" | Apposition → relative clause |
| "together with his wife and two sons" | "That man, his wife, and his two sons" | Pronoun resolution + list structure |
| "went to live for a while" | "moved... to live there for a few years" | Verb simplification + time specificity |
| "in the country of Moab" | "to a country named Moab" | "named" construction |
| (no title) | "Title: Elimelech and Naomi move..." | Title addition |

## Step 3: Reasons (Intermediary Layer)

1. **Title Addition**: Provides context summary for translators
2. **Temporal Simplification**: "When judges were ruling" clearer than "In the days when"
3. **Geographic Specificity**: "Israel" more explicit than "the land"
4. **Determiner Enhancement**: "A certain man" signals new character introduction
5. **Relative Clause**: "which was in Judah" explicit relationship vs apposition
6. **Pronoun Resolution**: "That man" replaces "a man" for clarity
7. **Verb Simplification**: "moved" more direct than "went to live"
8. **Time Specificity**: "for a few years" clearer than "for a while"
9. **Named Construction**: "country named Moab" explicit naming vs possessive

## Step 4: Rule Proposals

### Rule 1.1: Add Title
- **Pattern**: First verse of narrative sections gets title
- **Format**: "Title: [Summary of main event]"
- **Reason**: Provides context for translation

### Rule 1.2: Temporal Clause Simplification
- **Pattern**: "In the days when X" → "When X"
- **Reason**: Simpler temporal structure for translation

### Rule 1.3: Geographic Specificity
- **Pattern**: Generic "land" → specific "Israel" (when contextually clear)
- **Reason**: Explicit reference aids translation

### Rule 1.4: Determiner for New Characters
- **Pattern**: "a man" → "a certain man" (first introduction)
- **Reason**: Signals new character introduction

### Rule 1.5: Apposition to Relative Clause
- **Pattern**: "X in Y" → "X, which was in Y"
- **Reason**: Explicit relationship marker

### Rule 1.6: Pronoun Resolution in Lists
- **Pattern**: "a man... together with his" → "That man, his"
- **Reason**: Clear referent tracking

### Rule 1.7: Verb Simplification
- **Pattern**: "went to live" → "moved... to live"
- **Reason**: One action per verb phrase

### Rule 1.8: Time Specificity
- **Pattern**: "for a while" → "for a few years"
- **Reason**: Concrete time reference

### Rule 1.9: Named Construction
- **Pattern**: "country of X" → "country named X"
- **Reason**: Explicit naming relationship

## Step 6: Refined Rules

### R1: Title Addition
- Add title to first verse of narrative sections
- Format: "Title: [event summary]"
- Reference: notation.md §50

### R2: Temporal Simplification  
- Simplify temporal phrases: "In the days when X" → "When X"
- Reference: clauses.md (temporal clauses)

### R3: Geographic Specificity
- Replace generic "land" with specific name when contextually clear
- Pattern: "the land" → "Israel" (when Israel is meant)

### R4: Character Introduction Determiner
- Use "a certain [noun]" for first introduction of main characters
- Pattern: "a man" → "a certain man" (first mention)

### R5: Apposition to Relative Clause
- Convert apposition to explicit relative clause
- Pattern: "X in Y" → "X, which was in Y"
- Reference: clauses.md §4

### R6: Pronoun Resolution in Lists
- Resolve pronouns when listing participants
- Pattern: "a man... together with his" → "That man, his"
- Reference: pronouns.md

### R7: Verb Simplification
- Use simple verbs, avoid compound verb phrases
- Pattern: "went to live" → "moved... to live"
- Reference: notation.md §113

### R8: Time Specificity
- Replace vague time with specific when possible
- Pattern: "for a while" → "for a few years"
- Note: Only when source supports specificity

### R9: Named Construction
- Use explicit "named" for proper nouns
- Pattern: "country of X" → "country named X"
- Reference: notation.md §201
