# Ruth 1:22 Analysis

## Step 1: Source Texts

### NIV
> So Naomi returned from Moab accompanied by Ruth the Moabite, her daughter-in-law, arriving in Bethlehem as the barley harvest was beginning.

### Target Format (Expected Pattern)
> So Naomi went back from Moab. Ruth, [who was from Moab], went with Naomi. Ruth was Naomi's daughter-in-law. Naomi and Ruth arrived in Bethlehem [when people were beginning [to harvest barley]].

## Step 2: Differences Identified

| NIV | Target | Type |
|-----|--------|------|
| "returned" | "went back" | Verb simplification (R13) |
| "accompanied by Ruth" | "Ruth... went with Naomi" | Passive to active (R14) + sentence split (R2) |
| "Ruth the Moabite" | "Ruth, [who was from Moab]" | Apposition to relative clause (R11) |
| "her daughter-in-law" | "Ruth was Naomi's daughter-in-law" | Pronoun resolution + sentence split |
| "arriving in Bethlehem" | "arrived in Bethlehem" | Participle to verb (R2) |
| "as the barley harvest was beginning" | "[when people were beginning [to harvest barley]]" | Temporal clause + implicit subject (R33) |

## Step 3: New Rules Needed

### R34: Participle to Verb
- **Pattern**: Convert participles to main verbs
- **Example**: "arriving" → "arrived" (as main verb)
- **Reason**: Clearer verb structure
- **Priority**: Medium (covered by R2)

**Decision**: Covered by R2 - no new rule needed
