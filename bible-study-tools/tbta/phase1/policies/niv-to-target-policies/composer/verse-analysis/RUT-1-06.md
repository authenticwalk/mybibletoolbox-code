# Ruth 1:6 Analysis

## Step 1: Source Texts

### NIV
> When Naomi heard in Moab that the LORD had come to the aid of his people by providing food for them, she and her daughters-in-law prepared to return home from there.

### Target Format (Expected Pattern)
> When Naomi heard in Moab [that the LORD had helped his people by giving food to them], Naomi and her daughters-in-law prepared to go back to their home from there.

## Step 2: Differences Identified

| NIV | Target | Type |
|-----|--------|------|
| "the LORD had come to the aid of" | "the LORD had helped" | Idiom simplification |
| "by providing food for them" | "by giving food to them" | Verb simplification |
| "she" | "Naomi" | Pronoun resolution (R9) |
| "return home" | "go back to their home" | Verb + explicit location |

## Step 3: New Rules Needed

### R23: Idiom Simplification
- **Pattern**: Simplify idioms to direct verbs
- **Example**: "come to the aid of" → "helped"
- **Reason**: Direct action clearer for translation
- **Priority**: High

### R24: Verb + Preposition Simplification
- **Pattern**: Simplify verb + preposition combinations
- **Example**: "providing food for" → "giving food to"
- **Reason**: Simpler verb structure
- **Priority**: Medium

### R25: Location Explicit in Verb Phrases
- **Pattern**: Make location explicit in verb phrases
- **Example**: "return home" → "go back to their home"
- **Reason**: Explicit location reference
- **Priority**: Medium

## Step 4: Consolidation Check

- R23 (Idiom Simplification) - NEW, keep separate
- R24 (Verb + Preposition) - Could merge with R13 (Verb Simplification)
- R25 (Location Explicit) - Could merge with R16 (Geographic Expansion)

**Decision**: 
- Merge R24 into R13 (Verb Simplification)
- Merge R25 into R16 (Geographic Expansion)
- Keep R23 separate (Idiom Simplification)
