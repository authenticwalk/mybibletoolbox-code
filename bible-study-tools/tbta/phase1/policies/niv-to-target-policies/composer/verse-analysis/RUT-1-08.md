# Ruth 1:8 Analysis

## Step 1: Source Texts

### NIV
> Then Naomi said to her two daughters-in-law, "Go back, each of you, to your mother's home. May the LORD show you kindness, as you have shown kindness to your dead husbands and to me.

### Target Format (Expected Pattern)
> Then Naomi said to her two daughters-in-law, ["Go back, each of you, to your mother's home. May the LORD be kind to you, [as you were kind to your dead husbands and to me(Naomi)]."]

## Step 2: Differences Identified

| NIV | Target | Type |
|-----|--------|------|
| "show you kindness" | "be kind to you" | Idiom simplification (R23) |
| "as you have shown kindness" | "as you were kind" | Perfect tense simplification + idiom |
| "to me" | "to me(Naomi)" | First person referent marking |
| (no brackets) | [quote brackets] | Quote marking |

## Step 3: New Rules Needed

### R27: Perfect Tense Simplification
- **Pattern**: Simplify perfect tense when simple past works
- **Example**: "have shown" → "were" (when not recent/relevant)
- **Reference**: notation.md §18 (perfect tense)
- **Reason**: Simpler tense structure
- **Priority**: Medium

### R28: First Person Referent Marking
- **Pattern**: Mark first person referent in quotes
- **Example**: "to me" → "to me(Naomi)"
- **Reference**: pronouns.md (first/second person)
- **Reason**: Explicit referent in dialogue
- **Priority**: High

**Decision**: 
- R27 merges with R3 (Temporal Simplification) - tense is temporal
- R28 is NEW - keep separate for quote handling
