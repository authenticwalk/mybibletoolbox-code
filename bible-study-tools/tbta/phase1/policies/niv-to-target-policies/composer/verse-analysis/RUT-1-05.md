# Ruth 1:5 Analysis

## Step 1: Source Texts

### NIV
> both Mahlon and Kilion also died, and Naomi was left without her two sons and her husband.

### Target Format (Unchurched Adults)
> Then Mahlon and Kilion also died. So Naomi didn't have her sons or her husband.

### Target Format (Churched Adults)
> Then Mahlon and Kilion also died. So Naomi didn't have her sons or her husband.

## Step 2: Differences Identified

| NIV | Target | Type |
|-----|--------|------|
| "both Mahlon and Kilion" | "Mahlon and Kilion" | "both" removal |
| "died, and Naomi" | "died. So Naomi" | Sentence split |
| "was left without" | "didn't have" | Passive → active + negation |
| "her two sons and her husband" | "her sons or her husband" | "and" → "or" + "two" removal |

## Step 3: Reasons (Intermediary Layer)

1. **"both" Removal**: Redundant when names listed
2. **Sentence Splitting**: Separate events (same as R18)
3. **Passive to Active**: "was left without" → "didn't have" (simpler)
4. **Connector Change**: "and" → "or" (logical: didn't have either)
5. **Number Removal**: "two sons" → "sons" (number already established)

## Step 4: Rule Proposals

### Rule 5.1: Redundant Word Removal
- **Pattern**: "both X and Y" → "X and Y" (when redundant)
- **Reason**: Simplicity

### Rule 5.2: Sentence Splitting (repeat)
- **Pattern**: "X, and Y" → "X. So Y"
- **Reason**: Same as R18

### Rule 5.3: Passive to Active with Negation
- **Pattern**: "was left without" → "didn't have"
- **Reason**: Simpler active negation

### Rule 5.4: Logical Connector
- **Pattern**: "and" → "or" (in negative contexts)
- **Reason**: Accurate logic

### Rule 5.5: Established Number Removal
- **Pattern**: "two sons" → "sons" (when number already known)
- **Reason**: Avoid redundancy

## Step 6: Refined Rules

### R23: Redundant Word Removal
- Remove redundant words when meaning clear
- Pattern: "both X and Y" → "X and Y"
- Reason: Simplicity

### R24: Logical Connector in Negation
- Use "or" in negative contexts
- Pattern: "didn't have X and Y" → "didn't have X or Y"
- Reference: R16 (connector selection)

### R25: Established Number Removal
- Remove numbers when already established
- Pattern: "her two sons" → "her sons" (when "two" already mentioned)
- Reason: Avoid redundancy
