# Ruth 1:17 Analysis

## Step 1: Source Texts

### NIV
> Where you die I will die, and there I will be buried. May the LORD deal with me, be it ever so severely, if even death separates you and me."

### Target Format (Expected Pattern)
> Where you(Naomi) die, I(Ruth) will die. There I(Ruth) will be buried. May the LORD punish me(Ruth) severely if even death separates you(Naomi) and me(Ruth)."

## Step 2: Differences Identified

| NIV | Target | Type |
|-----|--------|------|
| "you" | "you(Naomi)" | Second person referent (R28) |
| "I" | "I(Ruth)" | First person referent (R28) |
| "and there" | "There" (sentence split) | Sentence splitting (R2) |
| "deal with me, be it ever so severely" | "punish me(Ruth) severely" | Idiom simplification (R23) |
