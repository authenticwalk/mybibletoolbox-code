# Ruth 1:11 Analysis

## Step 1: Source Texts

### NIV
> But Naomi said, "Return home, my daughters. Why would you come with me? Am I going to have any more sons, who could become your husbands?

### Target Format (Expected Pattern)
> But Naomi said, ["Go back to your home, my(Naomi's) daughters. Why would you(daughters) come with me(Naomi)? Will I(Naomi) have more sons, [who could become your(daughters') husbands]?"]

## Step 2: Differences Identified

| NIV | Target | Type |
|-----|--------|------|
| "Return home" | "Go back to your home" | Location explicit (R25/R16) |
| "my daughters" | "my(Naomi's) daughters" | First person referent (R28) |
| "you" | "you(daughters)" | Second person referent (R28) |
| "me" | "me(Naomi)" | First person referent (R28) |
| "Am I going to have" | "Will I(Naomi) have" | Future tense simplification |
| "any more" | "more" | Redundant word removal (R22) |

## Step 3: New Rules Needed

### R29: Future Tense Simplification
- **Pattern**: Simplify future tense constructions
- **Example**: "Am I going to have" → "Will I have"
- **Reason**: Simpler tense structure
- **Priority**: Low (covered by R13 Verb Simplification)

**Decision**: Covered by R13 - no new rule needed
