# Ruth 1:10 Analysis

## Step 1: Source Texts

### NIV
> and said to her, "We will go back with you to your people."

### Target Format (Expected Pattern)
> Those women said to Naomi, ["We(those women) will go back with you(Naomi) to your(Naomi's) people."]

## Step 2: Differences Identified

| NIV | Target | Type |
|-----|--------|------|
| "and said to her" | "Those women said to Naomi" | Pronoun resolution (R9) + sentence split (R2) |
| "We" | "We(those women)" | First person referent (R28) |
| "you" | "you(Naomi)" | Second person referent (R28) |
| "your people" | "your(Naomi's) people" | Second person possessive referent (R28) |

## Step 3: Consolidation

- R28 covers first/second person referents
- No new rules needed
