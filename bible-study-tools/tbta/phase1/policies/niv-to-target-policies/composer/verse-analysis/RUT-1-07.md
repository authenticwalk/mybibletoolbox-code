# Ruth 1:7 Analysis

## Step 1: Source Texts

### NIV
> With her two daughters-in-law she left the place where she had been living and set out on the road that would take them back to the land of Judah.

### Target Format (Expected Pattern)
> Naomi left the place [where she had been living] with her two daughters-in-law. Then Naomi and her daughters-in-law started traveling on the road [that would take them back to the region named Judah].

## Step 2: Differences Identified

| NIV | Target | Type |
|-----|--------|------|
| "With her two daughters-in-law she" | "Naomi... with her two daughters-in-law" | Pronoun resolution + reordering |
| "left... and set out" | "left... Then... started traveling" | Sentence splitting (R2) |
| "she" | "Naomi" | Pronoun resolution (R9) |
| "them" | "Naomi and her daughters-in-law" | Pronoun resolution (R9) |
| "the land of Judah" | "the region named Judah" | Named construction (R17) + geographic specificity |

## Step 3: New Rules Needed

### R26: Prepositional Phrase Reordering
- **Pattern**: Move prepositional phrases for clarity
- **Example**: "With X, she Y" → "She Y with X"
- **Reason**: Subject-first structure clearer
- **Priority**: Low (can be handled by R2 sentence splitting)

**Decision**: Covered by R2 (Sentence Splitting) - no new rule needed
