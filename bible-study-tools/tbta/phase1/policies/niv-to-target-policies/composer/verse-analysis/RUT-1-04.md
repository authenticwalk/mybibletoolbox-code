# Ruth 1:4 Analysis

## Step 1: Source Texts

### NIV
> They married Moabite women, one named Orpah and the other Ruth. After they had lived there about ten years,

### Target Format (Unchurched Adults)
> <<Naomi's sons grew up and became men.>> They married women who were from Moab. One son married a woman named Orpah. And the other son married a woman named Ruth. That family continued living in Moab for about ten years.

### Target Format (Churched Adults)
> Those sons married women who were from Moab. One son married a woman named Orpah. And the other son married a woman named Ruth. That family continued living in Moab for about ten years.

## Step 2: Differences Identified

| NIV | Target | Type |
|-----|--------|------|
| "They" | "Those sons" / "<<Naomi's sons grew up and became men.>> They" | Pronoun resolution + implicit addition |
| "married Moabite women" | "married women who were from Moab" | Adjective → relative clause |
| "one named Orpah and the other Ruth" | "One son married a woman named Orpah. And the other son married a woman named Ruth." | List decomposition + full sentences |
| "After they had lived there about ten years" | "That family continued living in Moab for about ten years." | Temporal clause → main clause + pronoun resolution |

## Step 3: Reasons (Intermediary Layer)

1. **Implicit Addition**: Add implicit growth/maturation for unchurched audiences
2. **Pronoun Resolution**: "They" → "Those sons" / "That family"
3. **Adjective to Relative Clause**: "Moabite women" → "women who were from Moab"
4. **List Decomposition**: Split into separate sentences (same as R13)
5. **Temporal Restructuring**: "After X" → "X" (main clause)
6. **Pronoun Resolution**: "they" → "That family"
