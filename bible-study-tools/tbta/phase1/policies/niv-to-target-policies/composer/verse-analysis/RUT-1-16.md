# Ruth 1:16 Analysis

## Step 1: Source Texts

### NIV
> But Ruth replied, "Don't urge me to leave you or to turn back from you. Where you go I will go, and where you stay I will stay. Your people will be my people and your God my God.

### Target Format (Expected Pattern)
> But Ruth said to Naomi, ["Do not tell me(Ruth) [to leave you(Naomi)] or [to turn back from you(Naomi)]. Where you(Naomi) go, I(Ruth) will go. Where you(Naomi) stay, I(Ruth) will stay. Your(Naomi's) people will be my(Ruth's) people. Your(Naomi's) God will be my(Ruth's) God."]

## Step 2: Differences Identified

| NIV | Target | Type |

| "replied" | "said to Naomi" | Verb + addressee explicit |

| "me" | "me(Ruth)" | First person referent (R28) |
| "you" | "you(Naomi)" | Second person referent (R28) |
| "Where you go I will go" | "Where you(Naomi) go, I(Ruth) will go." | Sentence splitting (R2) + referents |
| "Your people will be my people" | "Your(Naomi's) people will be my(Ruth's) people." | Sentence splitting (R2) + referents |
