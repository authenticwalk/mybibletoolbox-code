# Ruth 1:9 Analysis

## Step 1: Source Texts

### NIV
> May the LORD grant that each of you will find rest in the home of another husband." Then she kissed them goodbye and they wept aloud

### Target Format (Expected Pattern)
> May the LORD cause [each of you to find rest in the home of another husband]." Then Naomi kissed those women goodbye. Then Naomi and those women cried loudly.

## Step 2: Differences Identified

| NIV | Target | Type |
|-----|--------|------|
| "grant that" | "cause [that]" | Verb + complementizer removal (R11 pattern) |
| "she kissed them" | "Naomi kissed those women" | Pronoun resolution (R9) |
| "they wept aloud" | "Naomi and those women cried loudly" | Pronoun resolution + verb simplification |
| "wept aloud" | "cried loudly" | Verb simplification (R13) |

## Step 3: Consolidation

- All covered by existing rules (R9, R11, R13)
- No new rules needed
