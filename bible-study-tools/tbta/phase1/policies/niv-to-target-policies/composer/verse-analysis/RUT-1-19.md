# Ruth 1:19 Analysis

## Step 1: Source Texts

### NIV
> So the two women went on until they came to Bethlehem. When they arrived in Bethlehem, the whole town was stirred because of them, and the women exclaimed, "Can this be Naomi?"

### Target Format (Expected Pattern)
> So those two women continued traveling [until they came to Bethlehem]. When those women arrived in Bethlehem, all of the people in that town became excited [because of those women]. Those women in that town said, ["Is this Naomi?"]

## Step 2: Differences Identified

| NIV | Target | Type |
|-----|--------|------|
| "went on" | "continued traveling" | Verb simplification (R13) |
| "they" | "those women" | Pronoun resolution (R9) |
| "the whole town was stirred" | "all of the people in that town became excited" | Passive to active (R14) + expansion |
| "because of them" | "because of those women" | Pronoun resolution (R9) |
| "the women exclaimed" | "Those women in that town said" | Pronoun resolution + verb simplification |
| "Can this be" | "Is this" | Question simplification |

## Step 3: New Rules Needed

### R32: Question Simplification
- **Pattern**: Simplify question forms when possible
- **Example**: "Can this be" → "Is this"
- **Reason**: Simpler question structure
- **Priority**: Low (covered by R13)

**Decision**: Covered by R13 - no new rule needed
