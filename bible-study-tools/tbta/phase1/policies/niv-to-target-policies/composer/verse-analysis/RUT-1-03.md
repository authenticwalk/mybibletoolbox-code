# Ruth 1:3 Analysis

## Step 1: Source Texts

### NIV
> Now Elimelek, Naomi's husband, died, and she was left with her two sons.

### Target Format (Unchurched Adults)
> Later Elimelech, who was Naomi's husband, died. So Naomi lived with her two sons.

### Target Format (Churched Adults)
> Later Elimelech, who was Naomi's husband, died. So Naomi lived with her two sons.

## Step 2: Differences Identified

| NIV | Target | Type |
|-----|--------|------|
| "Now" | "Later" | Temporal connector change |
| "Elimelek, Naomi's husband" | "Elimelech, who was Naomi's husband" | Apposition → relative clause |
| "died, and she was left" | "died. So Naomi" | Sentence split + pronoun resolution |
| "was left with" | "lived with" | Passive → active + verb change |

## Step 3: Reasons (Intermediary Layer)

1. **Temporal Connector**: "Later" clearer than "Now" (relative time)
2. **Relative Clause**: Explicit relationship marker
3. **Sentence Split**: Separate events into separate sentences
4. **Pronoun Resolution**: "she" → "Naomi"
5. **Passive to Active**: "was left with" → "lived with" (simpler)

## Step 4: Rule Proposals

### Rule 3.1: Temporal Connector Precision
- **Pattern**: "Now" → "Later" (when indicating sequence)
- **Reason**: Clearer temporal relationship

### Rule 3.2: Apposition to Relative Clause (repeat)
- **Pattern**: "X, Y's Z" → "X, who was Y's Z"
- **Reason**: Explicit relationship (same as R1.5)

### Rule 3.3: Sentence Splitting
- **Pattern**: "X, and Y" → "X. So Y" (when separate events)
- **Reason**: One event per sentence

### Rule 3.4: Passive to Active
- **Pattern**: "was left with" → "lived with"
- **Reason**: Simpler active voice
