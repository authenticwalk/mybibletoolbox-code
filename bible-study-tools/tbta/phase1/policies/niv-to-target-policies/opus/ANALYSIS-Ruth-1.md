## Verse-by-Verse Breakdown

### Ruth 1:1

**NIV**: In the days when the judges ruled, there was a famine in the land. So a man from Bethlehem in Judah, together with his wife and two sons, went to live for a while in the country of Moab.

**Rules Applied**:

- R2: Split compound sentence
- R5: Numbers as digits (implicitly, "two" → "2")
- R6: Name pattern: "a country named Moab"
- R7: "Bethlehem in Judah" → "Bethlehem, which was in Judah"
- R11: "In the days when" → "When" (simplify idiom)
- R11: "in the land" → "in Israel" (make explicit)
- R11: "for a while" → "for a few years" (vague → specific)
- R18: Add title and footnote

**TBTA Output**: Title: Elimelech and Naomi move from Bethlehem to Moab. When judges were ruling Israel, there was a famine in Israel. A certain man was from Bethlehem, which was in Judah. That man, his wife, and his two sons moved from Bethlehem to a country named Moab to live there for a few years. Footnote: Before the Israelites had kings, the judges were the leaders of Israel.

### Ruth 1:2

**NIV**: The man's name was Elimelech, his wife's name was Naomi, and the names of his two sons were Mahlon and Kilion. They were Ephrathites from Bethlehem, Judah. And they went to Moab and lived there.

**Rules Applied**:

- R1: "They" → "Elimelech and Naomi", "That family"
- R2: One name per sentence
- R6: "a town named Bethlehem"
- R7: "Bethlehem, which was in <<the region named>> Judah"
- R8: "Ephrathites" → "in Ephrah's clan"
- R9: "The man's" → "That man's" (after mention)

**TBTA Output**: That man's name was Elimelech. Elimelech's wife's name was Naomi. One son's name was Mahlon. And the other son's name was Kilion. Elimelech and Naomi were in Ephrah's clan. That family was living in a town named Bethlehem, which was in <<the region named>> Judah. But they went to a country named Moab and lived there.
