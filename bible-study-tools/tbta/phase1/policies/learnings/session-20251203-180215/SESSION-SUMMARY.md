## Key Discoveries

### Vocabulary Patterns
1. "shortly" not in ontology → "[During the time [before...]]"
2. "Holy Spirit" → "Spirit of God" (Holy not recognized)
3. "opening" → "hole", "mat" → "bed" (not in ontology)
4. "paralyzed" → "not able [to move]" or "[who was not able [to walk]]"
5. "believers" → "people [who believed in Jesus]"
6. "bewildered/confused" → "surprised"
7. "together" → "in the same place"
8. "sackcloth" → "cloth/sackcloth" or "clothes [that were made from goat hair]"
9. "partner" → "friend/partner" (L2 pairing)
10. "refresh my heart" → "make my heart strong"

### Verb Case Frame Discoveries
1. "loved brother" triggers passive detection → use "loving brother" instead
2. "separate" has complex arguments → use "caused [to be away from]"
3. "begin/start" cannot take patient clauses → split into separate sentences
4. "write" always needs explicit patient → "write a letter" not "write this"
5. "give" + predicate adjective fails → use "cause [X to be happy]"

### Grammar Patterns
1. "could not" → "were not able [to...]" (modal verb ban)
2. "as" comparisons unsupported → use "like" or explicit statements
3. "if" conditionals → "Perhaps" or restructure
4. Aspect verbs (start/begin/stop) NEVER take brackets: "started sinking" correct
5. Hyphenated verbs always base form even with modals: "would come-up"
6. "where" cannot be relativizer → "the house [that X were in]"
7. Discourse "Now" → "At this time"

### Structural Patterns
1. Multi-sentence commands in quotes: split into separate `said` statements
2. "proclaimed a fast" → "decided [to not eat food]" or "told people [to not eat]"
3. "greatest to least" → "most important to least important"
4. "charge it to me" → "add that debt to my account" or "blame me"
5. Nested quotes with conditionals cause bracket-matching challenges

### Genre-Specific Observations
1. **Gospel**: Complex dialogue with walking/water vocabulary; aspect verbs common
2. **Acts**: Pentecost terminology needs Spirit of God construction; "tongues of fire" simile structure
3. **Prophetic**: Divine speech patterns; "40 days" numeric format
4. **Epistle**: Dense relational language; "as X" role constructions very problematic
