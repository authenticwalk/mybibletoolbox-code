# Ruth 2:2 - Quote Structure & Request Patterns

## Original NIV
"And Ruth the Moabite said to Naomi, 'Let me go to the fields and pick up the leftover grain behind anyone in whose eyes I find favor.' Naomi said to her, 'Go ahead, my daughter.'"

## Final He1
"One day Ruth [who was from Moab] said to Naomi, ["You(Naomi) (imp) let [me(Ruth) go to the fields]]. I(Ruth) will pick-up grain/barley [that people left in the fields]. I(Ruth) will work behind people [that will be kind to me(Ruth)]". Naomi said to Ruth, ["My(Naomi's) daughter, (imp) go"]."

## Key Patterns Learned

### 1. Multi-sentence quote structure
- `["First sentence]. Second sentence. Third sentence."`
- Bracket `["...]` only wraps the FIRST sentence (patient clause of "said")
- Remaining sentences continue inside the quote marks but outside the bracket

### 2. "Let me go" request pattern
- NIV: "Let me go..."
- He1: `You(Naomi) (imp) let [me(Ruth) go to the fields]`
- Structure: Imperative to listener to let speaker do action
- Inner patient clause `[me(X) go to...]` nested inside

### 3. Nationality expression
- NIV: "Ruth the Moabite"
- He1: `Ruth [who was from Moab]`
- Use relative clause, not "from a country named"

### 4. "Leftover grain" pattern
- NIV: "pick up the leftover grain"
- He1: `pick-up grain/barley [that people left in the fields]`
- "leftover" → relative clause explaining what was left
- Use pairing "grain/barley"

### 5. "Find favor" idiom
- NIV: "anyone in whose eyes I find favor"
- He1: `people [that will be kind to me(Ruth)]`
- Simplify idiom to "be kind to"

### 6. Scene beginning
- "One day" to mark beginning of scene (Begin Scene relation)

### 7. Hyphenated verbs
- "pick-up" not "pick up"
