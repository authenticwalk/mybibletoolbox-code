## Key Discoveries

### Structural Patterns
1. Multi-sentence quotes must be split: `said, ["S1."] said, ["S2."]`
2. `(title)` marker at narrative section starts
3. "(complex)/(simple)/(literal)/(dynamic)" for difficult concepts
4. `_paragraph` at section starts (NT)

### Vocabulary Patterns
1. "containers" not "jars"
2. "leader of the ship" not "captain"
3. "Masters-B/lords" for "Sirs"
4. "which things-B" not "what"
5. "Perhaps" not "Maybe"

### Grammar Patterns
1. "that" for people in relative clauses (He1), not "who"
2. "become thirsty" not "are thirsty"
3. "moved X's face to the ground" for prostration
4. "For" instead of "Because" at sentence start
5. Modal "must" NOT marked with (imp)

### Implicit Markers
1. `_implicitActiveAgent` for passive agents
2. `(implicit-info)` for He1 implicit
3. `(implicit-situational)` for contextual info
4. `_excl` for exclusive we
