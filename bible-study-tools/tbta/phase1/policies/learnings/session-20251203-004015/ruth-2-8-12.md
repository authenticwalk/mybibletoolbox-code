## Patterns Discovered
1. `(implicit-info) walked to X` before speech (Ru 2:8)
2. "that" for people in relative clauses, not "who" (Ru 2:8)
3. "become thirsty" not "are thirsty" (Ru 2:9)
4. "containers" not "jars" for water vessels (Ru 2:9)
5. "told [X to not verb Y]" for indirect commands (Ru 2:9)
6. "moved X's face to the ground" for bowing (Ru 2:10)
7. "the land [that you were born in]" for "homeland" (Ru 2:11)
8. Prayer: "I(X) will pray [that Yahweh will verb...]" (Ru 2:12)
9. Simile: "[just like X verbs Y]" then separate explanation sentence (Ru 2:12)
10. Quote bracket ends after first sentence only (Ru 2:8)
