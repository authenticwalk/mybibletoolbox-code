## Patterns Discovered
1. `_paragraph` marker at section starts (Ac 16:25, 16:26)
2. "(complex)/(simple)" dual translations for difficult concepts (Ac 16:26)
3. "bottom layer of stones" for foundations (Ac 16:26)
4. `_implicitActiveAgent` for passive agents (Ac 16:26, 16:30)
5. "opened by this event" - event as agent (Ac 16:26)
6. "the man [who was responsible for the prisoners]" for jailer (Ac 16:27)
7. "(footnote)" for extended implicit explanation (Ac 16:27)
8. "For" as causal connector instead of "Because" (Ac 16:27, 16:28)
9. `_excl` exclusive we marking with us(Paul) (Ac 16:28)
10. "still _implicit" for emphasis words (Ac 16:28)
11. "asked-B [X to bring Y]" request structure (Ac 16:29)
12. "(implicit-situational)" marker for contextual info (Ac 16:29)
13. "knelt in front of" not "fell before" (Ac 16:29)
14. "led...from that room" not "brought out" (Ac 16:30)
15. "asked-E" sense suffix for asking questions (Ac 16:30)
16. "Masters-B/lords" L2 pairing for "Sirs" (Ac 16:30)
17. "which things-B" not "what" (Ac 16:30)
18. Full salvation clause: "[in-order-to be saved by God _implicitActiveAgent from bad-B actions and death]" (Ac 16:30)
