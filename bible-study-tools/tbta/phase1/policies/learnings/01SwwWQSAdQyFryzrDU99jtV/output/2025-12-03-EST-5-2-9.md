## Patterns Discovered

### 1. Royal Court Vocabulary
- "scepter" → "stick/scepter" (L2) or just "stick" (L1)
- "court/courtyard" → "room" or "open area near the king's house"
- "banquet" → "meal" or "big-meal/banquet"
- "kingdom" → "land" or "land-ruled/kingdom"

### 2. Request/Petition Expressions
- "petition/request" (nouns) not in ontology
- Pattern: Use "pray [that...]" or "which thing do you want?"
- "What is your request?" → "Which thing do you want?"

### 3. Phrasal Verbs
- "hold out" → split to "held...out to" or "lifted...toward"
- "approach" → "came to" or "walked to"

### 4. Idiom Handling
- "find favor in your eyes" → "be kind to me" or "help me"
- "in high spirits" → "felt good" or "very happy"
- "filled with rage" → "anger filled X" (active restructure)

### 5. "Even" is Forbidden
- "Even up to half" → omit "even" per §0.16
- Restructure emphasis without forbidden words
