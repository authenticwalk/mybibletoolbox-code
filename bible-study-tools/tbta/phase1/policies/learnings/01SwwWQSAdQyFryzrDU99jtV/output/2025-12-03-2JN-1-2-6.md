## Patterns Discovered

### 1. "command" Verb Case Frame
- "command [that we do X]" fails (same-participant)
- Use "gave the rule [that...]" instead
- Or "told us(people) [to do this]"

### 2. "be with" Preposition
- "truth will be with us" → "truth [is in the hearts of us(people)]"
- Or "exist in hearts of"
- Preposition "with" may not be recognized

### 3. L3 Theological Terms
- "grace" → "gift" or "good things"
- "mercy" → "kind actions"
- "beginning" → "long-ago times"

### 4. "lady" Vocabulary
- "lady" not in ontology → use "woman"
- "dear lady" → "woman"

### 5. Truth/Love Abstract Terms
- "walk in truth" → "walk [the way the Father told us]"
- "walk in love" → "walk in the way of the Father"

### 6. Epistle Blessing Formula
- Omit complex blessing structures
- Restructure into explicit agent-verb-patient

## Issues Unresolved
- "command" verb case frame needs documentation
- Epistle complexity requires more iterations
- Theological vocabulary gap for L3 words
