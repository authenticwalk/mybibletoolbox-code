## Patterns Discovered

### 1. "Savior" Explication
- "Savior" → "person [who saves-C people]" (per V1 learnings)
- Or "[who saves people]" in relative clause

### 2. "blameless" Structure
- "blameless" → "have a good name" (concrete expression)
- Avoid abstract adjectives, use results

### 3. Elder/Overseer Requirements
- "must be X" → split into separate sentences
- One requirement per sentence

### 4. Negative Requirements
- "not overbearing" → "does not think he is better"
- "not quick-tempered" → "does not get angry fast"
- "not given to drunkenness" → "does not drink too much wine/alcohol"

### 5. "appoint" Verb
- "appoint elders" → "choose leaders"
- L1 simplification

### 6. Belief Verb Issue
- "believe" requires patient clause
- "children believe" fails → "children [who believe God]"

## Issues Unresolved
- V3 deixis format not recognized by current linter
- Complex epistle verbs (command, entrust) need restructuring
- Predicate adjective constraints
