## Patterns Discovered

### 1. Angel/Messenger Vocabulary
- "angel" (L2) → "messenger [who comes from God]"
- Or simply "man" for He1 level vocabulary
- Specific identity is theological context

### 2. Passive Voice Restructuring
- "your words were heard" → "God heard your words" (active)
- Add explicit agent when theologically clear

### 3. Quote Structure Limits
- Extended quotes spanning verses need breaking
- One-sentence quotes work better
- Pattern: Break multi-verse speech into blocks

### 4. Trembling/Shaking
- "trembling" → "shaking" (L1)
- Physical state description

### 5. Temporal Markers
- "21 days" (digits)
- "first day" → "first day" (ordinal OK)

### 6. Prince/Ruler Terms
- "prince of Persian kingdom" → "person [who rules a land]"
- Explication for political titles
