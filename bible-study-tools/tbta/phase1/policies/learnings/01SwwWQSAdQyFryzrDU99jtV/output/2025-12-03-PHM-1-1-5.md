## Patterns Discovered

### 1. Epistle Greeting Structure
- Split greeting into multiple sentences per recipient
- "fellow worker" → "worked with I(Paul)"
- "fellow soldier" → "fights with I(Paul)" or "one [who fought battles with them]"

### 2. "Church" Vocabulary
- "church" (L2) → "assembly" or "people gathered" or "group of believers"
- Pattern: Use concrete noun for abstract religious term

### 3. Grace/Peace Formula
- "Grace and peace" → "I(X) pray-hope [God will give a gift to...]"
- Split into separate sentences for gift and peace

### 4. Possessive Structures
- Never use "I(Paul)'s"
- Use "of I(Paul)" pattern
- "my God" → "God my(Paul) God" or "the God of I(Paul)"

### 5. "dear friend" Simplification
- "dear friend" → "friend" (remove "dear")
- Or use "good friend"

### 6. we(people) for Generic Deixis
- Compound deixis like "we(Paul-and-Timothy)" fails
- Use generic "we(people)" instead

## Issues Unresolved
