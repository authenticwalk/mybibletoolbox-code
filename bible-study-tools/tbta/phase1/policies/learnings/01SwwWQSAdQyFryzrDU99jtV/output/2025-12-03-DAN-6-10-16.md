## Winner

## Patterns Discovered

### 1. Complex Nested Quotes
- Long passages with multi-level quotes cause bracket matching errors in V2/V3
- V1's approach of keeping quotes simpler worked better
- Pattern: Split complex multi-sentence quotes when possible

### 2. Legal/Decree Vocabulary
- "decree/edict" → "law" or "rule" (L0-1)
- "repeal" → "change" or "make different"
- "in accordance with" → restructure to avoid

### 3. Geographic/Ethnic References
- "Medes and Persians" → "people [who live in Media and Persia]" or "people named Medes"
- Ethnic proper nouns may need expansion

### 4. Temporal Clauses with "where"
- "where the windows opened" → split: "that had windows" + separate sentence for facing direction
- "where" cannot be relativizer directly

### 5. L2 Pairings Confirmed
- upset/distressed
- prisoners/exiles
- tell/announce (for proclamation)
- serve/worship

## Issues Unresolved
- Linter bracket matching on complex nested structures (V2/V3 both struggled)
- "Law of the Medes and Persians" proper noun handling inconsistent
