# V1 Learnings (Policy-First)

Patterns learned from encoding with the policy-first approach.

## Process
Add learnings here when V1 wins or discovers a new pattern.

---

## Verb Structure
- Avoid "have" with adverbial clauses; use existential "be" ("there was not enough food")
- Use `gave birth to` (NOT "birth" or "birthed")
- Use `sexed` not "slept with"
- Use `came to X` not `arrived at X`
- Use `lived` not `living`
- Passive → active: "was being prepared" → "someone was making" (Ac 10:10)

## Numbers
- Use numerals (2, 3, 10) not words — number words not recognized as adjectives

## Ambiguous Words
- Add `_noun`, `_verb`, `_adj` to clarify ambiguous words like "judges"

## Patient Clauses
- Never use "that" at start: `[God gave food...]` not `[that God gave food...]`
- Quote structure: `said, ["First sentence"]. Second sentence...`

## L2 Word Pairings
- `worship` → `serve/worship`
- `swear` → `promise/swear`
- `harvest` → `gather/harvest`
- `barley` → `grain/barley`
- `workers` → `servants/workers`
- `bow` → `kneel/bow`
- `centurion` → `officer/centurion` (Ac 10:1)
- `vision` → `dream/vision` (Ac 10:3)
- `memorial` → `gift/memorial` (Ac 10:4)
- `servant` → `servant/worker` (Tit 1:1)
- `apostle` → `representative/apostle` (Tit 1:1)
- `elders` → `leaders/elders` (Tit 1:5)
- `message` → `words/message` (Tit 1:3)
- `Savior` → `person [who saves-C people]` (Tit 1:3)

## L2 Word Substitutions
- "Magi" → "wise men" (Mt 2:1)
- "overjoyed" → "very happy" (Mt 2:10)

## Explications
- `daughter-in-law` → `son's wife`
- `mother-in-law` → `husband's mother`

## Family/Clan
- Use "was in X's family/clan" for membership

## Names
- Prefer "a [noun] named [name]" over separate sentence

## Title Positioning
- "King Herod" → "Herod the king" or "king Herod" — lowercase title (Mt 2:1)

## Nationality
- "X the Moabite" → `X [who was from Moab]`

## Idioms
- "find favor in eyes" → `be kind to me`
- "leftover grain" → `grain/barley [that people left]`
- "The Lord be with you" → `I pray [that Yahweh will be with you]`
- "May the Lord..." → `I pray [that Yahweh will...]`

## Hyphenated Verbs
- Hyphenated verbs don't inflect: `pick-up` not `picked-up`
- Two-word verbs DO inflect: `sat down`, `stood up`

## Quote Structure
- Multi-sentence: bracket first sentence only (Mt 2:8)
- Nested: `["outer ["inner"]"]`

## Request Patterns
- "Let me go" → `You(X) (imp) let [me(Y) go to...]`

## Time Expressions
- "at about 3 in the afternoon" → `[when the time was about 3 during the afternoon]` (Ac 10:3)

## Proper Noun Markers
- Foreign proper nouns need `_noun`: "Italian _noun Regiment _noun" (Ac 10:1)

## Verb Case Frames
- "have" with children → ontology rejects (Tit 1:6)
- "believe" intransitive → rejected, needs patient (Tit 1:6)
- "say [that X is Y]" → rejected, need alternatives (Tit 1:9)
- "write" MUST have patient: "write a letter" (Phm 1:1)
- "give" with beneficiary → use "be kind" + "cause [to have peace]" (Phm 1:3)
- "believe/trust" cannot be intransitive, needs patient clause (Phm 1:5)

## Epistle Complexity
- "must be X, must be Y" → split into separate sentences (Tit 1:6)
- Purpose clauses → [so...will] structure (Tit 1:5)

## Possessive Structures
- NEVER use "I(Paul)'s" → use "of I(Paul)" (Phm 1:1)
- "my God" → "the God of I(Paul)" (Phm 1:4)

## Unrecognized Connectors
- "So", "But", "though", "concerning" → use "However" or restructure (Phm 1:8)

## Epistle Greetings
- Split complex greetings into multiple sentences (Phm 1:1)
- "fellow worker" → "work with I(Paul)" (Phm 1:1)
- "fellow soldier" → "fights with I(Paul)" (Phm 1:2)

## L3 Words
- "beginning" → "long-ago times" or "old" (2Jn 1:5)
- "grace" → "gift" (2Jn 1:3)
- "mercy" → "kind" (2Jn 1:3)

## Deixis Constraints
- "elder" is L2 → "leader" (can't pair in deixis) (2Jn 1:1)
- "lady" not recognized → "woman" (2Jn 1:1)
- "antichrist" → "enemy of Christ" (2Jn 1:7)
- "teaching" → "words/teaching" (2Jn 1:9)

## Epistle Prayer Structure
- "Grace and peace" → `I(X) pray-hope [God will give a gift to...]` + `I(X) pray-hope [God will cause [X to have peace]]` (2Jn 1:3)

## Command Case Frames
- "Father commanded us [we must...]" FAILS (same-participant) (2Jn 1:4)
- Solution: "Father commanded [people must...]" (generic patient)

## Narrative Structure
- Use `(title)` marker at narrative section starts (Jon 1:1)
- Use "One day" opener for narrative beginnings (Jon 1:1)
- "decided [to verb]" for intent/purpose (Jon 1:3)

## Causative Constructions
- "caused [X to verb]" for divine actions on nature (Jon 1:4)

## Vocabulary Patterns
- "heavy things" not "cargo" for ship goods (Jon 1:5)
- "leader of the ship" not "captain" (Jon 1:6)
- "Perhaps" not "Maybe" for possibility (Jon 1:6)
- "men [who worked on that ship]" for sailors (Jon 1:5)

## Geographic Names
- Name seas explicitly: "Mediterranean Sea" (Jon 1:4)

## Implicit Markers (He1)
- Use `(implicit-info)` for implicit information (Jon 1:5)

## Famine/Scarcity Expression
- "there was a famine" → "many people [who were living in X] did not have enough food" (Ru 1:1)
- Use active subject "many people did not have" not existential "there was not"

## Determiner Patterns
- First mention of specific individual: "A certain man" not just "a man" (Ru 1:1)
- Subsequent reference: "That man" not "the man" (Ru 1:1)

## Title Clause Names
- Title clause may use character names from later verses (Ru 1:1)
- Pattern: `Name (title) and Name verb from Place to Place.` (Ru 1:1)
