# V2 Learnings (Evidence-Based)

Patterns learned from encoding with the reverse-engineering approach (6,963 verse corpus).

## Process
Add learnings here when V2 wins or discovers a new pattern.

---

## Verb Structure
- Avoid "have" with adverbial clauses; use existential "be" ("there was not enough food")

## Numbers
- Use numerals (2, 3, 10) not words — number words not recognized as adjectives

## Ambiguous Words
- Add `_noun`, `_verb`, `_adj` to clarify ambiguous words like "judges"

## Patient Clauses
- Never use "that" at start: `[God gave food...]` not `[that God gave food...]`
- Quote structure: `said, ["First sentence"]. Second sentence...`

## L2 Word Pairings
- `worship` → `serve/worship`
- `swear` → `promise/swear`
- `harvest` → `gather/harvest`
- `barley` → `grain/barley`
- `workers` → `servants/workers`
- `bow` → `kneel/bow`

## Explications
- `daughter-in-law` → `son's wife`
- `mother-in-law` → `husband's mother`

## Verb-Specific
- Use `gave birth to` (NOT "birth" or "birthed")
- Use `sexed` not "slept with"
- Use `came to X` not `arrived at X`
- Use `lived` not `living`

## Family/Clan
- Use "was in X's family/clan" for membership

## Names
- Prefer "a [noun] named [name]" over separate sentence

## Nationality
- "X the Moabite" → `X [who was from Moab]`

## Idioms
- "find favor in eyes" → `be kind to me`
- "leftover grain" → `grain/barley [that people left]`
- "The Lord be with you" → `I pray [that Yahweh will be with you]`
- "May the Lord..." → `I pray [that Yahweh will...]`

## Hyphenated Verbs
- Hyphenated verbs don't inflect: `pick-up` not `picked-up`
- Two-word verbs DO inflect: `sat down`, `stood up`

## Quote Structure
- Multi-sentence: bracket first sentence only
- Nested: `["outer ["inner"]"]`

## Request Patterns
- "Let me go" → `You(X) (imp) let [me(Y) go to...]`
