# Pronouns (Checklist §3)

## Core Rule
**Do not use third person pronouns** (he, she, it, they, him, her, them) except for "each-other".

## First & Second Person
Must have referent in parentheses:
- `I(Paul)`, `you(people)`, `we(Peter)`
- Use simple word (L0-1) or name (L4) in parentheses
- ❌ `you(disciples)` — L2 word
- ❌ `you(Peter and John)` — multiple people
- ✅ `you(Peter)` — use first person for multiple

## Inclusive/Exclusive
For "we", "us", "our" — mark with `_incl` or `_excl`:
- `We(Peter) _excl are not bad` — excluding hearer
- `We(Peter) _incl should go` — including hearer

## He1 Relaxation
- First occurrence per verse: use full notation `I(Paul)`
- After first: can use natural pronouns
- This helps AI understand referents

## Each-Other
The only allowed third-person-like pronoun:
- `each-other(people)` ✅
- Used for reciprocal actions

## Examples

| NIV | He1 |
|-----|-----|
| "He went to the town" | "John went to the town" |
| "They said to him" | "The Pharisees said to Jesus" |
| "She was left with her sons" | "Naomi lived with Naomi's sons" |
| "We are not guilty" (Paul to Jews) | "We(Paul) _excl are not guilty" |

## Common Mistakes

| Wrong | Right | Why |
|-------|-------|-----|
| "he went" | "John went" | No 3rd person pronouns |
| "you(followers/disciples)" | "you(followers)" | No pairings in parentheses |
| "we went" (no marker) | "we(X) _incl went" | Must mark inclusive/exclusive |
