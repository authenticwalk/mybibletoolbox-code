# Clauses (Checklist §4-7)

## Core Rules

1. **One verb per clause** (auxiliaries like "do", "will" don't count)
2. **Subordinate clauses in brackets**: `[who was in the house]`
3. **Main clause never bracketed** (except first sentence of quote)
4. **Max 4 levels of nesting** — simplify if more
5. **Left brackets = right brackets** in each sentence

## Clause Types

### Independent (Main) Clause
- Has subject + verb
- Not in brackets
- `John went to the town.`

### Subordinate Clauses
All in brackets:

| Type | Example |
|------|---------|
| Relative | `the man [who was in the house]` |
| Patient/Object | `John knew [Mary was there]` |
| Adverbial | `[When John arrived] Mary left` |
| Purpose | `John went [in order to see Mary]` |

## Relative Clauses

**Must have relativizer**: `who`, `whom`, `that`
- ✅ `the man [who was in the house]`
- ❌ `the man in the house` (means "was small while in house")

**Prepositions must appear**:
- ✅ `the time [that John left at]`
- ❌ `the time [that John left]`

**No "when" or "where" as relativizers**:
- ❌ `the time [when John saw Mary]`
- ✅ `the time [that John saw Mary at]`
- (But "when" OK for adverbial: `[when John arrived]`)

**No possessive relativizers**:
- ❌ `the man [whose cat was black]`
- ✅ `the man [who had a black cat]`

**Restrictive vs Descriptive**:
- Common noun → restrictive (default)
- Proper noun → descriptive (default)
- Override: `that person [_descriptive who lived in Bethlehem]`

## Patient Clauses

**No "that" at start**:
- ✅ `John knew [Mary was there]`
- ❌ `John knew [that Mary was there]`
- (TBTA interprets "that" as demonstrative)

**Subject deletion rules**:
- Same as main subject: `John wanted [to eat]` (not `[John to eat]`)
- Same as main patient: `John wanted [Paul to eat]` (not `John wanted Paul [Paul to eat]`)
- Unrelated: `John knew [Paul was eating]`

## Adverbial Clauses

**Placement matters for meaning**:
- ✅ `We know [Jesus died because of our sins]` — we know the reason
- ❌ `We know [Jesus died] because of our sins` — we know because of our sins

**"When" vs "After"**:
- If action clearly follows, use "after"
- `[After John saw Jesus] John was happy`

## Purpose Clauses

**Use "in order to"** (not just "to"):
- ✅ `John went [in order to see Mary]`
- ❌ `John went to see Mary`

**Same agent required for "in order to"**:
- ✅ `John went [in order to see Mary]` — John sees
- ❌ `John went [in order to be seen by Mary]` — passive breaks rule

**Different agent? Use "so that"**:
- `John went [so that Mary could see John]`

## Counting Levels

```
John saw [Mary eating with the man [who went [in order to see Paul]]]
         └─ Level 1              └─ Level 2    └─ Level 3
```

If you hit 4 levels, break into multiple sentences.

## Common Mistakes

| Wrong | Right | Why |
|-------|-------|-----|
| `knew [that X]` | `knew [X]` | No "that" in patient clauses |
| `man in the house` | `man [who was in the house]` | Need relative clause |
| `time [when X]` | `time [that X at]` | No "when" as relativizer |
| `went to see` | `went [in order to see]` | Explicit purpose |
