# Test Verses for SKILL Comparison

## Test Case 1: Ruth 1:1

**NIV:**
> In the days when the judges ruled, there was a famine in the land. So a man from Bethlehem in Judah, together with his wife and two sons, went to live for a while in the country of Moab.

**Reference He1:**
> Elimelech (title) and Naomi move from Bethlehem to Moab. [When judges were ruling Israel] many people [who were living in Israel] did not have enough food. A certain man was from Bethlehem [which was in Judah]. That man, that man's wife, and that man's two sons moved from Bethlehem to a country named Moab [in order to live in Moab for a few years].

**Key challenges:**
- Title insertion
- "famine" → "did not have enough food"
- Relative clause for location
- "in order to" purpose clause
- "for a while" → "for a few years"

## Test Case 2: Ruth 1:3

**NIV:**
> Now Elimelek, Naomi's husband, died, and she was left with her two sons.

**Reference He1:**
> Elimelech [who was Naomi's husband] died later. So Naomi lived with only Naomi's two sons.

**Key challenges:**
- Apposition → relative clause
- "was left with" → "lived with only"
- Pronoun resolution ("she" → "Naomi")
- Temporal marker "later"

## Test Case 3: Ruth 1:4

**NIV:**
> They married Moabite women, one named Orpah and the other Ruth. After they had lived there about ten years,

**Reference He1:**
> Naomi's sons grew up. And Naomi's sons became men. Then these sons married women [that were from Moab]. One son married a woman named Orpah. The other son married a woman named Ruth. [after Naomi's sons married women], Naomi lived in Moab for ten years with Naomi's sons.

**Key challenges:**
- Pronoun resolution ("they" → "Naomi's sons")
- "Moabite women" → "women [that were from Moab]"
- Implicit information expansion (grew up, became men)
- Temporal clause structure
- Name introduction pattern

---

## Scoring Criteria

For each test case, score 0-5 on:

1. **Pronoun Resolution** (0-5): Are all pronouns properly resolved?
2. **Vocabulary** (0-5): Are complex words simplified/paired correctly?
3. **Clause Structure** (0-5): Are subordinate clauses marked with brackets?
4. **Implicit Info** (0-5): Is necessary implicit information added?
5. **Natural Flow** (0-5): Does it read naturally while being TBTA-compliant?

**Total possible: 75 points (25 per verse × 3 verses)**
