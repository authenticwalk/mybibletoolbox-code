## Key Patterns Applied

1. **Coreference**: All pronouns → explicit referents
2. **Named formula**: "a/the [type] named [Name]"
3. **Brackets**: All subordinate clauses in [...]
4. **Deixis**: I(Speaker), you(Addressee), we(Group), my(Possessor's)
5. **Speech**: ["..."]  for direct quotes
6. **Imperatives**: (imp) before verb
7. **Discourse markers**: And, Then, But, Because
8. **Past tense**: Keep natural past tense (was, came, heard, etc.)
9. **"Area"** instead of "region" (complexity issue)
10. **"Important priest"** instead of "chief priest" (complexity)
