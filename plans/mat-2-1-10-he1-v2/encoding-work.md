# Matthew 2:1-10 He1 Encoding (V2)

## Verse-by-Verse Transformation

### VERSE 1
**NIV**: "After Jesus was born in Bethlehem in Judea, during the time of King Herod, Magi from the east came to Jerusalem"

**Step 1 (Coreference)**: No pronouns to resolve

**Step 2 (Segment)**: Keep as one sentence (temporal clause + main clause)

**Step 3 (Brackets)**:
- Temporal: "[After Jesus was born in Bethlehem in Judea, during the time of King Herod]"
- Main: "Magi from the east came to Jerusalem"

**Step 4 (Named)**:
- First mention: "a man named Jesus" OR keep "Jesus" (known entity)
- "a town named Bethlehem"
- "a region named Judea"
- "the king named Herod"
- "a city named Jerusalem"

**Step 5 (Speech)**: No speech

**Step 6 (Simplify)**: "Magi" → "wise-men" (common pattern)

**Step 7 (Discourse)**: No discourse marker (first clause)

**Step 9 (Learnings)**: Apply patterns

**He1 Draft**: "[After Jesus was born in a town named Bethlehem [which was in a region named Judea], during the time of the king named Herod], wise-men from the east came to a city named Jerusalem."

### VERSE 2
**NIV**: "and asked, 'Where is the one who has been born king of the Jews? We saw his star when it rose and have come to worship him.'"

**Step 1 (Coreference)**:
- "his" → "that one's" OR "the king of the Jews'"
- "him" → "that one" OR "the king of the Jews"

**Step 2 (Segment)**:
- "and asked" → "And the wise-men asked"
- Split compound in quote: "We saw X" + "we have come"

**Step 3 (Brackets)**:
- Relative: "[who has been born king of the Jews]"
- Temporal: "[when it rose]"
- Purpose: "[in order to worship that one]"

**Step 4 (Named)**: Already covered

**Step 5 (Speech)**:
- Quote: ["..."]
- "we" → "we(wise-men)"

**Step 7 (Discourse)**: "And" (continuation)

**He1 Draft**: "And the wise-men asked, ["Where is the one [who has been born king of the Jews]? We(wise-men) saw that one's star [when it rose]. And we(wise-men) have come [in order to worship that one]."]"

### VERSE 3

### VERSE 6
**NIV**: "'But you, Bethlehem, in the land of Judah, are by no means least among the rulers of Judah; for out of you will come a ruler who will shepherd my people Israel.'"

**Step 1 (Coreference)**:
- "you" (x2) → reference to Bethlehem (personification - keep as is)
- "my" → "my(God's)" OR "my(prophet's)"

**Step 2 (Segment)**: Multiple clauses to split

**Step 3 (Brackets)**:
- Relative: "[who will shepherd my people Israel]"
- Causal: "[because...]"

**Step 4 (Named)**: "a people named Israel"

**Step 5 (Speech)**: This is quoted prophecy (nested quote)

**Step 7 (Discourse)**: "But" (contrast), "because" (reason)

**He1 Draft**: "["But you(Bethlehem), Bethlehem [which is in the land of Judah], are by no means least among the rulers of Judah. Because a ruler [who will shepherd my(God's) people named Israel] will come out of you(Bethlehem)."]"

### VERSE 8

**Step 3 (Brackets)**:

- Purpose: "[so that I too may go and worship the child]"

**Step 5 (Speech)**: ["..."], imperatives with (imp)

**Step 6 (Simplify)**: "search carefully" → "search carefully" (keep)

**Step 7 (Discourse)**: Multiple "And" connectors

[As soon as you(wise-men) find the child], you(wise-men) (imp) report to me(Herod), [so that I(Herod) too may go and worship the child]."]"
